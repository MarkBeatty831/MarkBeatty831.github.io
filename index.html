About Me

I am a sophomore studying Finance at the David Eccles School of Business at the University of Utah. My interest in finance comes from wanting to understand how a business actually creates value, and I plan to use that foundation as an entrepreneur, building and running my own company rather than only analyzing someone else's. Outside of coursework I spend a lot of time on artificial intelligence. I use AI tools daily for research, writing, and problem solving, and my longer-term goal is to train a model of my own so I understand the technology from the inside instead of only as a user. On the technical side I am strongest in Excel and PowerPoint, and I am currently learning HTML, CSS, and Git so I can build and publish my own work. I care most about clear communication, dependable follow-through, and leading teams that finish what they start.
